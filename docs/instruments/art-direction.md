# Chain Dials art direction

One page a reviewer can check a screenshot against. Written before the review
harness, following the Course Builder playbook (`docs/instruments/graphics-playbook.md`).
Reference captures live in `docs/instruments/reference/` and are regenerated by
`tests/browser/review-harness.spec.ts`. Nothing in this document is proof the
dials look finished. Only a visual review on the harness is.

## Identity in one sentence

**Precision bench instruments for a dark room: one lamp, one glass, one reading
per dial, readable from a sofa and honest about what it does not know.**

## What every instrument must do

- **One reading.** The number in the frame header is the instrument. The art
  explains it. If the art disagrees with the number, the art is wrong.
- **Presentation never changes a reading.** Visual layers read store data and
  write none. The harness report flags any reading that differs between accent
  and neutral light (`readingDrift` must stay empty).
- **Unknown is not zero.** Null data shows an "awaiting" or "unavailable" state
  with no needle, flame, sand or bar. See the "No data" scenario captures.
- **Still by default.** Motion is a block arriving, a hand moving, or a slow
  ambient breath. Nothing pulses forever to look alive. Reduced motion freezes
  everything and keeps the reading.

## Light

- **One key light, up and to the left.** `LIGHT_RIG.key` in `src/lib/dial-light.ts`
  (40 %, 30 % of the lit shape). Bezel metal, faces, hubs, glass and the frame
  sheen all take their highlight from it.
- **Metal rims run light to dark along the top-left to bottom-right diagonal**
  (`LIGHT_RIG.rim`).
- **Contact shadow under every bezel**, 3 units down at 35 % (`LIGHT_RIG.shadow`).
- **Emissive sources keep their own light**: forge core and slag, burn flame,
  fog bands, block-found flashes. They glow; they are not lit.
- **No bloom to hide defects.** Soft blur filters are allowed only as a thin halo
  on an accent stroke, and they are dropped on the mobile tier. If a dial only
  reads well with its glow on, the dial is unfinished. Check it in neutral light.
- **No depth of field, no vignette heavier than the frame's own.**

## Shape rules per instrument

Written as do / don't, like the playbook's "no cone stacks".

### Cadence dials (Block Metronome, Slot Lattice, Turbine Tach, Clearing Clock)

- Do: a single hand or a single lit segment; tick ring with majors every fourth
  tick; the reference interval printed on the face.
- Don't: two competing hands, a progress ring plus a hand showing the same
  number, a hand that freezes at full scale. Overdue laps become studs on the
  bezel, not a second ring.

### Fee weather (Mempool Atmosphere, Base Fee Tide, Priority Jets, Funding Tide)

- Do: altitude means fee rate everywhere; one legend; samples as small
  bright points that arrive and leave.
- Don't: a flat colour wash with no bands, camera-facing sprites that spin,
  particles that never die. Fog is WebGL when available and the 2D sky when
  not; both must read the same.

### Block glyphs (Block Sigil, Block Mosaic, Leader Ribbon, Hash Tape)

- Do: every byte of the hash contributes; the glyph changes only when the tip
  changes; click copies the hash.
- Don't: decorative noise unrelated to the hash, animation between blocks,
  a glyph that reads the same for different hashes at a glance.

### Issuance (Issuance Hourglass, Burn Candle, Inflation Fountain, Volume Fountain)

- Do: a vessel with a level, a logarithmic scale where the data spans decades
  and says so on the face (`LOG`), sand or spray only on a block pulse.
- Don't: a continuous pour, a flame with no scale, a full vessel at zero.

### Security (Hashrate Forge, Validator Constellation, Stake Reef, OI Vault)

- Do: heat or density as the single driver; epoch or orbit as a secondary ring.
- Don't: a glow blob with no structure (the "balloon on a pole" failure); stars
  that twinkle on a loop with no data behind them.

## Quality tiers

`src/lib/dial-light.ts`, applied by `useQualityTier()`.

| | Desktop | Mobile (`pointer: coarse` or ≤ 2 GB memory) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Bezel ticks | as requested (48–72) | halved, majors kept on multiples of four |
| Soft SVG blur filters | on | off |
| WebGL fog | on | on, pixel ratio capped at 2 |
| Readings and geometry | identical | identical |

The harness forces a tier with `data-quality-tier` on `<html>`.

## Acceptance views

All captured by the harness spec and kept in `docs/instruments/reference/`.
Judge on these, not on the live board.

| View | Viewport | What it must prove |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Board, accent | 1440×900 | the intended mood |
| Board, neutral | 1440×900 | nothing depends on the accent glow |
| Board, bare art | 1440×900 | each dial reads without frame glass |
| Board, late tip | 1440×900 | the ordinary miss: warn and stale tones |
| Board, no data | 1440×900 | unavailable states, no fake zeros |
| Board, mobile | 390×844 (Pixel 7) | mobile tier, no horizontal overflow |
| Wall | 1920×1080 | TV distance |
| Stage 01 and 03 | 1920×1080 | the close view where defects show |
| Live motion | any | hand moves, fog drifts, nothing pulses forever |

A capture passes only when a person says so. Tests prove bounds, determinism
and that readings are untouched. They say nothing about taste.
