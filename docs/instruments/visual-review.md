## Open findings, by severity

1. **SOL and HYPE invent readings when data is missing.** In the No data
   scenario, Inflation Fountain shows `40.0%`, Stake Reef `40% reef`, OI Vault
   `40% vault`, Volume Fountain draws a basin, and Funding Tide draws a synthetic
   sine wave. ETH shows "Awaiting" states for the same scenario. This breaks the
   bible's "unknown is not zero" rule. Files: `sol/InflationFountain.tsx`,
   `sol/StakeReef.tsx`, `hype/OiVault.tsx`, `hype/VolumeFountain.tsx`,
   `hype/FundingTide.tsx` (the `?? 0.4`, `?? 40` and `Math.sin` fallbacks).
   See `sol-board-empty.png`, `hype-board-empty.png`.
2. **Hash Tape clips the hash.** On the HYPE board and stage the 64 digits run
   off the right edge of the tape (`hype-stage-03.png`). Either scroll the tape
   or size the box to the string.
3. **Inflation Fountain and Volume Fountain read as a grey box with a stick in
   neutral light.** With the accent gone there is no structure: no basin
   level, no spray geometry, no scale. The forge-family rule applies: structure
   first, glow second. `sol-board-neutral.png`, `sol-board-bare.png`.
4. **Leader Ribbon is a text box.** Bare art shows a monospace string in a
   rounded rectangle. It needs a glyph that changes with the hash, like Sigil
   and Mosaic do. `sol-board-bare.png`.
5. **Issuance Hourglass sits small in a wide frame.** On the 1440 board the
   glass occupies a third of its cell's width with empty glass on both sides.
   Either widen the composition (scale or secondary ring) or narrow the cell.
6. **Funding Tide and Priority Jets have no scale on the face.** The ETH Tide
   prints its gwei axis; the HYPE and SOL weather panels print only legends.
7. **Block Mosaic keeps a blue hue under neutral light** because its hue is
   hash-derived HSL, not the accent. Acceptable under the palette rule (variation
   inside a material) but it is the only dial that stays coloured; decide
   whether that is intended.

## What reads well

- Block Metronome, Slot Lattice, Tach and Clock read in neutral light and in
  bare art. The late-tip tone shift (warn, then stale red) is unmistakable.
- ETH is the most complete board: every instrument has an unavailable state,
  a scale, and holds its shape without the accent.
- Stage views at 1920×1080 are clean, opaque and centred.
- Mobile at 390 wide has no horizontal overflow on any chain.
